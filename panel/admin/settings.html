Send emails

Master switch. Turn OFF to stop every order email instantly.

Install the Trigger Email from Firestore extension in Firebase Console (Extensions). Set the collection name to mail and add your Gmail SMTP details there. Needs the Blaze plan. The site only writes documents to mail; passwords stay inside Firebase.

EmailJS setup (5 min): create a free account at emailjs.com → Email Services → add Gmail → copy the Service ID. Email Templates → new template: To = {{to_email}}, Subject = {{subject}}, Content (HTML) = {{{message_html}}} → copy the Template ID. Account → copy the Public Key. In EmailJS also add your website domain under allowed origins. These three values are public by design.
